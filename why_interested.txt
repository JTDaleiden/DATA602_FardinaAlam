Why are you interested in taking DATA602?
    I am interested in taking DATA602 so that I can broaden my understanding on the basic fundamentals of data science. Not only what data science entails, but how to apply it to the data life cycle and work through data analysis from start to finish. Utilizing data to make data-driven decisions has been something I take pride in with my work through the Coast Guard, but I am getting the opportunity to work with larger data sets and more raw data to make service-wide decisions. Improving my skills at each step through the data life cycle will ensure that the analysis and data-driven decisions I will make in the Coast Guard are in the best interest of the service and supported with the knowledge gained through this class.
